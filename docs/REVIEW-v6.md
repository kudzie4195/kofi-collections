## CATCHES

1. **D2 — migration drops a secondary medium tag on a seeded work.** Claimed: "seeded works tagged" the same as the seed/live patch. True: on a v5 device, `aw_tobias_1` (Crowd (Twelve Vessels), medium `installation`, seed tags `['medium.ceramics','medium.installation',…]`) migrates to `['medium.installation','style.contemporary','theme.community','material.clay','display_format.freestanding']` — `medium.ceramics` is lost, so the Ceramics filter/facet differs from a fresh install and from the Supabase live patch. Cause: `src/data/mock/MockDataSource.ts:2102` takes the *first* `medium.*` id in the seed list as the primary mirror (`seed.tagIds.find((id) => id.startsWith('medium.'))`), but library order puts Ceramics before Installation. Repro: `npx vitest run --config review-v6/vitest.probe.config.mts mock.probe` → R14 fails with exactly this diff. Fix: compare against the seed work's own medium (store `medium` in `SEED_CLASSIFICATION` or look it up from `buildSeed()`), i.e. drop `mediumTagId(seedMedium)` only when `a.medium !== seedMedium`. Only this one work is affected (priya_1/2: printmaking sorts first; deshawn_2: video is the only medium id).

2. **A1 — keyboard focus is lost after removing an active filter chip or pressing Clear all.** Claimed: chips with remove are keyboard-operable. True: the button works, but after Enter on `Remove filter: Painting` (one of three) or on `Clear all`, `document.activeElement` is `<body>` at 390 and 1280 (kbd2.mjs). The removed button unmounts and nothing takes focus; a screen-reader/keyboard user is thrown to the top of the page. `src/features/gallery/GalleryScreen.tsx:124–136` (`commit(c.without)` / `commit(clearedFilters(filters))`). Fix: after commit, focus the next remaining chip, else the Filters button (`kg-controls` button), via a ref + effect.

3. **C1 — a claim the product cannot back.** `src/features/studio/tagCopy.ts:9` (`TAG_COPY.intro`): "…a handful that honestly describe the work will bring it up in more of the searches that matter to you than a long list would." Mechanically false: filters are OR within a category and AND across, with no relevance penalty for tag count, so a work with more tags matches *more* searches, never fewer (src/domain/filters.ts:85–93, tagFacets). The sentence is persuasive copy dressed as a mechanism. Reword to what is true, e.g. "Pick what fits. Curators search by these tags; a few honest ones put the work in the right searches. Up to 6 per group, 4 for Social Impact."

4. **P1 — public-page boundary text is a tooltip only.** Claimed: boundary text shown at the picker and on the public page. True at the picker (visible lede, TagPicker.tsx:177–183). On `/work/:id` the workbook sentence "Tag artwork content/intent, not artist identity or verified social results." is only in `title=` on the row and on each chip (`src/features/gallery/ArtworkScreen.tsx:318, :329`); the visible lede is "Chosen by the artist to describe the work.", which says "artist-chosen" but not "not a verified outcome". Same pattern in the filter sheet (`FilterSheet.tsx:338`). On phones (the demo's primary surface) the tooltip never appears. Fix: render the boundary (or a short public form of it, e.g. "What the work is about, as the artist states it — not a verified result.") as visible small text under the Social Impact row and group.

5. **D1 (note, not blocking) — the live patch rewrites `updated_at` on all 13 demo works.** The patch header says "No inserts, no deletes, no other columns", and 0011's own in-migration data fix explicitly disables `artworks_touch` to keep `updated_at` unchanged (0011_taxonomy.sql:869–881), but `apply/0011_live_patch.sql` runs the UPDATEs with the touch trigger live. livepatch.probe.sql (patch applied in a rolled-back transaction after resetting the columns): `bumped_after = 13`, 0 audit rows. Effect: `ArtistsScreen.tsx:26` sorts an artist's works by `updatedAt`, so after the patch every demo artist's lead work becomes a tie on the same timestamp (ordering depends on sort stability). Fix: wrap the patch's UPDATE block with `alter table kofi.artworks disable/enable trigger artworks_touch`, as the migration does.

Minor notes (no action required): matching sentence "This work is memory." reads slightly oddly for a tag name; "This work is tagged Memory." would read better. `matchCopy.editor.mediumHint` and `effectiveCriteria` are consistent. The filter sheet has both "Materials" (tags) and "Materials as listed" (free text) groups, which a visitor may not distinguish.

## GAPS (out of scope; the ledger must state them)

- **Pagination / virtualisation (spec-attack 13, workbook P1 "500+ works remains responsive"):** none in the Works grid, the filter sheet, `MatchList`, or `PartnerCallReview`. In Supabase mode `search_artworks` has `p_limit/p_offset` and server facets, but `SupabaseDataSource.listArtworks` calls it with `p_limit: null` and `GalleryScreen` always fetches the whole visible set and computes facets client-side. Measured with 300 extra works in the mock (scale.mjs, headless desktop Chromium): Works page 309 cards in 740 ms, one chip toggle in the sheet 486 ms (facet recompute), Works-that-fit 152 rows in a 29,707 px document. Nothing breaks at this size, but a 486 ms tap on a desktop is a second-plus on a phone; a 50–200-applicant demo will be scrollable but not snappy. Stated gap, not a catch.
- Phase 2 curatorial tags, Phase 3 impact evidence, custom-keyword moderation/aliases: not built (spec-attack 11). The SQL has `tags.aliases`/`deprecated_at` columns and a relabel test; nothing in the UI uses them.
- CSV import has no tag column: `importCsv` writes free-text `tags` (keywords) only; imported works get the medium mirror and nothing else (spec-attack 12). The workbook's `tag_custom_keywords` is Phase 2.
- The live patch tags only the 13 fictional demo works by UUID; real artists' works get their medium tag only (0011 data fix) — correct per spec-attack 10, and should be said in the ledger.
- Matching ships in v6 although the workbook files "Matching / recommendations" under Phase 3 (P2). It respects the workbook's test ("no sensitive identity assumptions": inputs are tagIds/uses/locationLabel/dimensions only).
- `matchesFilters` region filter still uses the artist's region label (`artistRegionLabel`), while matching uses the work's `locationLabel`; a work stored elsewhere filters by its artist's region on /works. Pre-existing behaviour, consistent with the filter's label "Location" meaning the artist.
- The filter sheet is ~4,576 px tall at 390 (19 groups). Facet counts for structured groups and tag groups are computed on every draft change over the whole visible set (`tagFacets` is O(categories × works)).

## Consensus reads (non-binding)

**Q1 — Tagging as Mara at 390.** It reads as picking, not form-filling: eight serif-headed groups of hairline chips, "1 of 6" counters, the main medium pre-pressed and explained, "Show all 25" per group, and a search box at the top; I tagged a work in well under two minutes in the walk. Filtering as a visitor feels like browsing when the medium strip and the active-chip bar are in play, but the sheet itself is a 4,500 px column of nineteen groups with two groups both called "Materials", so once you open it the database shows through; I would collapse the structured groups (Orientation, Edition, Available for, Career stage, Experience, Budget, Year) under one "More" disclosure and merge "Materials as listed" into the Materials group as a typed-input row.

**Q2 — Kofi Noir.** The picker, the pressed cream chip with the gold focus ring, the facet counts in the sheet and the ranked "Works that fit" rows with check/cross reasons hold the bar; the fit pill in gold against the serif title is the best new element. Two things fall short of 9+: every definition except Social Impact lives in a tooltip, so on a phone the chips are bare labels (a one-line "What these mean" disclosure per group, as Social Impact has, would fix it), and the match sentences' "This work is memory." lower-cases a proper label mid-sentence. I would also cap the sheet's Show-all expansion so a group never pushes 25 chips below the fold without its heading.

## Verdict

**FIX THEN SHIP.** Everything the workbook asks for is implemented in all three layers and I could not break the privacy or OR/AND rules in the mock or in SQL. The five catches are each a few lines: fix 1 (migration primary-medium lookup), 2 (focus after chip removal), 3 (the intro claim) and 4 (visible boundary on the public page) before the demo; 5 (live patch `updated_at`) before the patch is pasted into the live project. The pagination gap must be stated in the ledger, not fixed for v6.
